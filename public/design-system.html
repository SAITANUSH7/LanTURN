Design system

LanTURN v2 is Dark Soul / Liquid Glass: a black foundation, white type, a single ice-blue energy, and frosted iOS-style glass surfaces with soft specular highlights. Everything is built from the CSS variables in tokens.css, so changing a token restyles the whole app.

Black first

The page is --void. White is for text, icons and highlights only, never for large surfaces.

Glass, not boxes

Surfaces are translucent with a blur, a 1px light edge and an inner top highlight. A highlight follows the pointer.

Black, white, one ice blue

White pills are the primary action. Ice blue highlights data and links. Red appears only for errors.

Colour

White on --void is 19:1. --text-2 is 9:1 and --text-3 is 6:1 on black. Status colours are always paired with an icon or a word, never colour alone.

Do

Use verbs: “Upload resume”, “Post a job”. Pair icons with text.

Don’t

Put two accent buttons side by side, or use danger for anything reversible.

Accessibility rules

RuleHow it is met
ContrastBody text 4.5:1 or better; large text 3:1. Tokens were picked to meet this.
Colour is never aloneSkill state uses icon + text + border style. Status badges include an icon and a label.
FocusEvery control shows a 2px black + ice-blue ring on keyboard focus.
TargetsButtons are at least 44px tall (36px for compact tables and chips).
Motionprefers-reduced-motion turns animation off; prefers-reduced-transparency swaps glass for solid panels.
StructureSkip link, landmarks, one h1 per view, labelled form fields, live regions for results and toasts.
